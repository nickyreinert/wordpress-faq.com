Community Q&A

Why is Beaver Builder not loading on my WordPress site?

It is often caused by a conflict with your active theme or another plugin. Try switching to a default theme to test.

How do I troubleshoot Beaver Builder errors?

Check your browser console for JavaScript errors, disable all plugins except Beaver Builder, and clear your site cache.
