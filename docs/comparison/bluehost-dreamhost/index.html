Is Bluehost or Dreamhost better for WordPress?

Both are officially recommended by WordPress.org, but Dreamhost generally offers better performance and a more straightforward pricing structure.

Do Bluehost and Dreamhost include free SSL?

Yes, both hosting providers include free Let’s Encrypt SSL certificates with all their WordPress hosting plans.
