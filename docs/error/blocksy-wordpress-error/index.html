Why is my Blocksy theme not working correctly?

Common causes include browser or server-side caching, outdated theme files, or conflicts with other installed WordPress plugins.

How do I fix Blocksy theme errors?

Start by clearing your site cache, updating the Blocksy theme and companion plugin, and disabling recently added plugins to identify conflicts.
