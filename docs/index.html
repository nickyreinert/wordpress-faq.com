Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bluehost better than Namecheap for WordPress?
Bluehost is officially recommended by WordPress.org, while Namecheap is often praised for its budget-friendly pricing and domain management tools.
Does Cloudways offer a free migration service?
Yes, Cloudways provides a free migration service for your first site and offers a dedicated WordPress Migrator plugin for self-service transfers.
Why is my WordPress site slow on Cloudways?
Slow speeds on Cloudways are often caused by the default Breeze caching plugin, DNS propagation delays, or high Ajax request overhead.
Why is my WordPress site slow on SiteGround?
Slow performance is typically caused by heavy themes like Elementor, excessive plugin usage, or unoptimized images rather than the host.
What is the main difference between WordPress.org and WordPress.com?
WordPress.org is self-hosted software you install on your own server, while WordPress.com is a managed hosting service provided by Automattic.
Is SiteGround good for WordPress?
Yes, SiteGround is widely regarded as a solid hosting provider for WordPress, offering features like 1-click staging, daily backups, and responsive support.
