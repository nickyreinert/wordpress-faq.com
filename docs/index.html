Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my WordPress site slow on SiteGround?
Most slowness is caused by site-specific factors like heavy themes, too many plugins, or unoptimized images rather than the hosting environment.
Why is my WordPress site slow on WP Engine?
Slow performance is usually caused by inefficient database queries or excessive script loading from plugins and themes, not the host.
Is WP Engine good for WordPress hosting?
WP Engine is a highly regarded managed WordPress host known for performance and security, though some users have recently expressed concerns regarding support.
Is SiteGround good for WordPress?
Yes, SiteGround is generally regarded as a high-quality host for WordPress, known for its helpful support and integrated tools like staging and backups.
How do I migrate my WordPress site to SiteGround?
You can use migration plugins like Duplicator or WPvivid, or perform a manual migration by exporting your database and files via cPanel.
Does Kinsta offer free WordPress migration?
Yes, Kinsta provides free migrations performed by their engineering team for all hosting plans.
