Frequently Asked Questions

Is WP Engine good for WordPress hosting?
WP Engine is a premium managed WordPress host known for high performance and security, though some users report recent frustrations with support.
Why is my WordPress site slow on SiteGround?
Most speed issues are caused by bloated themes, too many plugins, or unoptimized images rather than the hosting environment.
Why is my WordPress site on Bluehost so slow?
Slow speeds are often caused by excessive plugins, unoptimized images, bloated themes, or server-level resource limitations on shared hosting plans.
Why is my WordPress site slow on WP Engine?
Slow performance is usually caused by inefficient themes or plugins making excessive database queries rather than the hosting provider.
Why is my Contact Form 7 not working?
It is often caused by a conflict with another plugin, a caching issue, or a database error following a site migration.
Why is my WordPress site slow on Kinsta?
Slow loading is often caused by unoptimized images, heavy plugins, or database bloat rather than the hosting environment itself.
