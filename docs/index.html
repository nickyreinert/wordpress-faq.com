Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Contact Form 7 not working?
It is often caused by a conflict with another plugin, a caching issue, or a database error following a site migration.
Is SiteGround good for WordPress?
Yes, SiteGround is generally well-regarded in the WordPress community for its ease of use, reliable support, and built-in tools like staging and backups.
Why is my WPForms plugin not working?
WPForms may stop working due to version mismatches, plugin conflicts, or outdated dependencies. Always check for pending updates in your dashboard.
How do I fix a fatal error caused by Yoast SEO?
If you cannot access the admin panel, use FTP or your hosting file manager to rename or delete the 'wordpress-seo' folder inside /wp-content/plugins/.
Why is Beaver Builder causing a fatal error?
Fatal errors are often triggered by conflicts with third-party plugins like kvCore IDX, which can bloat error logs and crash the site.
Why is my site showing 'Crawled but not indexed' in Google Search Console?
This often occurs due to low-quality content, duplicate pages, or incorrect canonical tags set within the Yoast SEO plugin settings.
