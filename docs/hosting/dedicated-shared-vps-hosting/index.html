Community Q&A

What is the difference between shared, VPS, and dedicated hosting?

Shared hosting shares resources with other sites, VPS provides virtualized dedicated resources, and dedicated hosting gives you an entire physical server.

Which WordPress hosting type is best for beginners?

Shared hosting is generally the best choice for beginners due to its low cost and ease of use for small to medium-sized websites.

When should I upgrade from shared to VPS hosting?

You should upgrade when your website experiences high traffic, requires specific server configurations, or needs more consistent performance.
