Community Q&A

Is Gutenberg compatible with custom themes?

Yes, Gutenberg is designed to work with any theme, though block-based themes offer the most seamless experience for layout customization.

How do I add Gutenberg support to my theme?

You can add support by including theme.json files and registering block styles within your functions.php file.
