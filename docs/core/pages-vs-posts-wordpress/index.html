Community Q&A

What is the main difference between a post and a page in WordPress?

Posts are dynamic, chronological entries often used for blogs, while pages are static, timeless content used for information like About or Contact pages.

Can I use categories and tags on WordPress pages?

By default, WordPress pages do not support categories or tags, as they are designed for hierarchical, static content rather than chronological feeds.

Which should I use for my homepage?

Most users prefer a static page for their homepage to control the layout, though you can set your latest posts as the homepage in reading settings.
