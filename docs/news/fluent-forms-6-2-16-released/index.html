Fluent Forms 6.2.16 Released

high confidence based on 1 community posts

What’s new in Fluent Forms 6.2.16

Released: (Date: October 16, 2026)

Active installations: 800,000

Tested up to WordPress 7.1.3

Requires PHP 7.4

Changelog

  • Adds “Add my past orders” to claim guest orders by email
  • Adds a smart code picker to the Custom HTML field
  • Adds MCP tools to the WordPress Abilities REST API
  • Fixes missing emails on payment forms without a Payment Method field
  • Fixes a fatal error in the [ff_entry] shortcode
  • Fixes entry export including all fields when none are selected
  • Fixes a redundant aria-label on field labels
  • Fixes wrong field types for Dropdown and Simple Text from AI and MCP
  • Restricts [ff_entry] to entry viewers and the submitter
  • Links guest orders only after email confirmation
  • Rejects invalid payment amounts, quantities, and checkbox values
  • Tightens permission checks on theme settings in shortcodes and blocks

Previous releases

6.2.15 — (Date: September 30, 2026)

  • Adds a Stripe Payment Element option to the inline Stripe field, with Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Adds an Autocomplete setting on form fields so browsers can fill in details like name, email, and address
  • Adds a Duplicate option for integration feeds
  • Adds an Export option to the Entries page
  • Adds an optional per-form AI Agents (WebMCP) setting that describes your form to AI-powered browsers, turned off by default

6.2.14 — (Date: September 15, 2026)

  • Improves Stripe payment confirmation when updates arrive after checkout
  • Improves payment retries and checkout confirmation messages
  • Improves subscription trial, installment, and custom amount processing
  • Improves refund and completed payment consistency
  • Refines payment totals for conditional quantities and inventory-controlled items
