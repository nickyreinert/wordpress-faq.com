Community Q&A

How do I add a custom widget to my WordPress sidebar?

Navigate to Appearance > Widgets in your dashboard, then drag and drop the desired block or widget into your active sidebar area.

Can I create my own custom widget?

Yes, you can create custom widgets by registering them in your theme’s functions.php file or by using a custom block plugin.

Why are my widgets not showing up on the site?

This is often caused by browser caching, plugin conflicts, or an incompatible theme; try clearing your cache and disabling conflicting plugins.
