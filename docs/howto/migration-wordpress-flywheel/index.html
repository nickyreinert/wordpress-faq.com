Community Q&A

How do I migrate my WordPress site to Flywheel?

You can use the Flywheel Migrations plugin or submit a migration request through your Flywheel dashboard for their team to handle it.

Does Flywheel support multisite migrations?

Yes, but manual intervention is often required; it is recommended to contact Flywheel support before initiating a multisite transfer.
