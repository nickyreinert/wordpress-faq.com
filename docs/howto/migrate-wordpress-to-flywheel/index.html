Community Q&A

How do I migrate my WordPress site to Flywheel?

You can use the Flywheel Migrations plugin or request a free migration from their support team by providing your current site credentials.

Do I need to manually move my database to Flywheel?

No, Flywheel’s migration service handles the database transfer automatically to ensure all site settings and content are preserved correctly.
