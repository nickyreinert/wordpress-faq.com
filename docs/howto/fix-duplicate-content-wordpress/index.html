Community Q&A

How do I fix duplicate content in WordPress?

You can fix duplicate content by using canonical tags, setting up 301 redirects, and ensuring your site structure avoids redundant archive pages.

Does duplicate content hurt WordPress SEO?

Yes, duplicate content can confuse search engines, potentially leading to lower rankings for your primary pages.
