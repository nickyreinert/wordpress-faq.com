Community Q&A

Does Kinsta offer free WordPress migration?

Yes, Kinsta provides free migrations performed by their expert team for all hosting plans.

How long does a WordPress migration to Kinsta take?

Most migrations are completed within 24 to 48 hours depending on the size and complexity of your website.

Can I migrate my WordPress site manually to Kinsta?

Yes, you can migrate manually using tools like Duplicator or by importing your database and files via SFTP.
