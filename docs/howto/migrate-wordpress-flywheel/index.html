Community Q&A

How do I migrate my WordPress site to Flywheel?

You can use the Flywheel migration plugin or request a free migration from their support team by providing your current site credentials.

Do I need database access to migrate to Flywheel?

Yes, having your database username, password, and host information is critical for a successful manual migration or troubleshooting.
