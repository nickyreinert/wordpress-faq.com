Community Q&A

How do I point my domain to Namecheap hosting?

Log into your domain registrar, navigate to DNS settings, and update the nameservers to match the values provided in your Namecheap hosting welcome email.

Do I need to move my files before changing nameservers?

Yes, it is recommended to upload your WordPress files and import your database to the new server before updating DNS to ensure zero downtime.
